Project KS2P (Knowledge Share 2π)

We live in a world of constant technological innovations. More and more information and opportunities become readily available. Be it current world affairs, educational insights, career advice, job opportunity, sustainable development or other areas of expertise. However, the main challenge is that the internet is flooded of irrelevant information and how we can to make relevant information available for the relevant people. This challenge includes the language barriers as information is often only available in dominant speaking languages such as English.

To overcome these challenges my colleague Jázon Szabó and I are building a world-wide platform which connects people seeking knowledge with experts who can speak their native tongue and who perhaps have been in the same boat or who just want to contribute their own knowledge in order to help. This platform acts as a gateway for the aforementioned idea which will drive the younger generation into a better future with extensive help from mentors across the world.

Knowledge is powerful and is a way out of poverty.

We will further showcase a beta of this version at UN Headquarter in New York in September 2016 during Merit360.
