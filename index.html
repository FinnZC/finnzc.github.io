Executor of Merit360 Summer 2016 - New York, USA

Merit360 is the world's most significant program for young people to tackle the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals. This program will bring together 360 changemakers from all over the world who will work in teams and develop action plans to tackle each of the 17 goals. As part of World Merit’s partnership with the United Nations, these action plans will then be presented to the United Nations at its headquarters in New York City with the objective of paving our way to successfully addressing these challenges before or by 2030. I am a Merit360 Delegate and part of writing and implementing Action Plan 001 for United Nation Sustainable Goal Number 1: No Poverty.

Project KS2P (Knowledge Share 2π)

Knowledge Share 2 Pi (aka KS2P) is focused on providing a secure social and professional worldwide platform to help people reach their potential and empower young people and encourage multicultural interaction, thus promote global entrepreneurship. These objectives are obtained by providing users relevant source of information from peers and mentors from around the world. Topics are limitless, thus encourage users to discover and learn. This will be an open platform that connects people to the relevant peers and mentors which allows social and professional networking, thus allowing long term mentoring, peer to peer support and progress monitoring. The main idea is to share knowledge, encourage cross-border communication, self-development and find the right connection for the right problem.

My colleague Jázon Szabó and I are dedicating our time alongside various other programmes in building this novel platform for the greater good.

Knowledge is powerful and is a way out of poverty.

We will further showcase a beta of this version at UN Headquarter in New York in September 2016 during Merit360.
